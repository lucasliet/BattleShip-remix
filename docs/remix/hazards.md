# Smash Remix stage hazards

Covers how the Remix stage hazards (`Hazards.asm`, the `Stages.function_table` dispatch, the hazard items and projectiles) are implemented
in the port. Code: `decomp/src/gr/grremix/` (one `grremix<stage>.c` plus a `grremixhazards_<group>.c` dispatcher per group), the gates in
`decomp/src/gr/grcommon/`, the hazard items in `decomp/src/it/itremix/`. Test: `SSB64_VS_STAGE=<decimal id>` with
`.claude/skills/remix-smoke-tests/scripts/stage_sweep.sh` (load and crash check of ids 41 to 215) and `hazard_sweep.sh <id>...` (4000 frames
with a contact sheet, hazards act on timers up to about frame 3600); `SSB64_REMIX_TOGGLES="Hazard Mode=1"` turns the hazards off.

## 1. Dispatch and conventions

### 1.1 From a stage id to a hazard routine

`Stages.function_table[gkind]` (Stages.asm:3185) holds one of: a vanilla ground maker address, `function.CLONE`, `OS.NULL`, or a `Hazards.*`
setup routine. In the port the gkind is the Remix stage id (`grremixids.h`, generated by `tools/gen_remix_stage_ids.py`) and
`grMainSetupMakeGround` calls `grRemixMakeGround` (`grremixstage.c`) for ids above 0x28. It reads `class_table` and `function_table` from
the Remix RAM image and does:

1. Class BTT or BTP (`bonus_fix_1_`): `sc1PBonusStageMakeBonus1Ground` or `sc1PBonusStageInitBonus2`, whatever the function says.
2. A vanilla maker address (the nine `GRREMIX_FUNC_*` values: Peach's Castle 0x8010B4AC, Sector Z 0x80107FCC, Congo Jungle 0x80109FB4,
   Planet Zebes 0x80108448, Hyrule Castle 0x8010AB20, Yoshi's Island 0x80108C80, Dream Land 0x801066D4, Saffron City 0x8010B2EC, Mushroom
   Kingdom 0x80109C0C): the matching `gr*MakeGround`.
3. `GRREMIX_FUNC_CLONE` (0x801056F8, a `jr ra`): nothing, 75 stages have no hazard code of their own.
4. Anything else: `grRemixHazardsMakeGround(gkind, function)` (`grremixhazards.c`) asks each `grRemixHazard<Group>` in turn. A group
   matches `function == REMIX_SYM_Hazards__<label>` (symbols from `port/remix/remix_symbols.h`, generated by `tools/gen_remix_actions.py`
   from the `Hazards.` prefix) and returns TRUE. An unmatched routine logs `[remix-stage] gkind=0x.. hazard routine 0x.. is not ported`.

Stages that reuse a vanilla maker keep its hazards and Remix patches them inside the vanilla function (section 2). Stages with a `Hazards.*`
routine run it instead of a vanilla maker. Reading the `function_table` entries of the Remix build gives these groups:

### 1.2 Hazard Mode and Movement

`Toggles.entry_hazard_mode` (title `Hazard Mode`): 0 Normal, 1 Hazards off, 2 Movement off, 3 All off. It only applies on the VS and Training
screens; elsewhere hazards and movement are both on. Two sets of accessors:

- `grRemixHazardsAreOn()` / `grRemixMovementIsOn()` (`grremixhazards.c`) for the Remix routines.
- `grRemixHazardToggleIsOn()` / `grRemixMovementToggleIsOn()` (`grremixgating.c`) for the gates in the vanilla makers; they return TRUE
  outside VS and Training.
- `grRemixHazardsMatchHasStarted()`: TRUE in Training, otherwise `time_passed != 0` (the asm test of `Global.match_info` elapsed time). Hazards
  that wait for the match clock use it (the water of Great Bay, Twilight City, Pirate Land and Japes; the minion factory) or an identical local
  test (the bee, Cacodemon and Banzai factories, the Onett car).

### 2.1 Platform movement rule

`platform_movement_` starts the platform animation process of a stage unless the stage has the MOVEMENT bit in `Stages.stage_hazard_table`
(NONE 0, HAZARDS 1, MOVEMENT 2, BOTH 3) and movement is off. Onett (0x49), Tal Tal Heights (0x2E) and Tal Tal Heights Remix (0xA7) follow
the hazard toggle instead of the movement toggle. The port decides it in `grRemixPlatformMovementIsOn` (`grremixyoshiclouds.c`). Deviation: the
asm reads `Stages.stage_hazard_table`, which `add_stage` fills for every Remix stage; the port keeps a copy of the first 11 entries (ids 0x00 to
0x0A), and every other id counts as having no MOVEMENT bit, so Movement off only stops the platforms of those 11 stages.

### 2.2 Yoshi's Island clouds

With hazards off the asm sets `DObj.user_data` to 4 on yakumono DObjs 1, 2 and 3 (lines 1, 2, 3 of `dGRYosterCloudLineIDs`), which disables
the cloud clipping, and then jumps to the end of the function, so no clouds exist. The port writes the same value and then continues into
`grYosterInitAll`, which re-enables the lines through `mpCollisionSetYakumonoOnID`: the clouds remain with hazards off.

### 2.3 Dream Land family

`grremixdreamland.c` (Whispy Mode, `Toggles.entry_whispy_mode`: Normal, Japanese, Super, Hyper) hooks `grpupupu.c`:

| Asm | Hook | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `dreamland_downtime_initially_` | `grPupupuInitAll` | Hyper: first wait 0x100 frames, else the vanilla random wait |
| `dreamland_downtime_` | `grPupupuWhispyUpdateStop` | Hyper: stop wait 0x25 frames |
| `dreamland_wind_speed_max_` | `grPupupuWhispySetWindPush` | Normal and Japanese 6.0 (vanilla), Super and Hyper 30.0 |
| `dreamland_wind_speed_multiplier_` | same | distance decay Normal 0.0006, Japanese 0.0003, Super and Hyper 0.0006 (the vanilla US value); the 1P game keeps the vanilla value |
| `dream_land_wind_parameters` | `grRemixDreamLandGetWindBox` | wind box scale 1.09375 (Super) and 1.1953125 (Hyper); the Dream Greens edges are +-3811 (top 1000, bottom -10), equal to the vanilla constants |

Deviations: the Super branch of `dreamland_wind_speed_max_` compares the Japanese id with the mode (`t2` is never reloaded), so Super never
takes the 20.0 value and gets 30.0; the port keeps that. The "1P" test of the asm is `Global.current_screen == TITLE_AND_1P`, a screen id
shared with the title; the port tests `nSCKind1PGame`.

Dream Greens (0x95) keeps its Whispy, flower and map head offsets in its own table: `dream_greens_hardcoding_1-4` select the map head offset
(0x158) and four map GObj descriptors in `grPupupuInitAll` under `grPupupuIsDreamGreens()`; `dream_greens_hardcoding_animation_1-4` read the
Whispy eye and mouth animation and texture tables from `stages/dream_greens_offsets.bin` in the Remix RAM image (offsets 0x00, 0x20, 0x60, 0x78).

## 3. Stage hazards

Ids are hex (decimal for `SSB64_VS_STAGE`).

### 3.1 Great Bay 0x36 (54), Twilight City 0xBA (186): water

Asm `great_bay_setup` / `twilight_city_setup` and `*_water_`. Port: `grRemixWaterMakeGround(water_y, splash_y)` (`grremixwater.c`), called from
`grremixhazards_baydraculatwilight.c` with water y -1128 / splash y -828 (Great Bay) and 0 / 0 (Twilight City, the asm values are placeholders).
Per fighter (ports 0 to 3), skipped when the match has not started, and for Fox, J Fox and Falco in Fire Fox Air (action 0xE8): the first frame
below `water_y` spawns damage sparks on both sides at `splash_y` and plays FGM 0x3D1; every frame under water the aerial velocity is multiplied
by 0.9375 on x and 0.875 on y (the y value is stored only when the result is below -20), the knockback velocity by 0.98046875 on x and y (y
additionally by 0.9375 when below -20), and the fast-fall, absorb and `is_goto_attack100` bits are cleared (the asm clears the whole bitfield
byte with mask 0x07; kept as written).

### 3.2 Dracula's Castle 0xB0 (176), Reverse Castle 0xB1 (177), Grim Reaper's Cavern 0xBE (190)

`draculas_castle_setup_` and `reapers_setup_` write the global fighter env colour (`set_player_env_colour_`, RAM 0x80131388 =
`gMPCollisionLightColor`, read by `mpCollisionSetLightColorGetAlpha`): 0xFFA8C4FF for the two castles, 0xFF0000FF for the cavern when hazards
are on. Deviation: the `function_table` of the Remix build gives 0xBE `function.CLONE`, so `reapers_setup_` is never called by the game; the
port implements it and it stays unreachable.

### 3.3 Corneria 0x46 (70): Great Fox

Asm `corneria_setup` and `great_fox_`. Port: `grremixcorneria.c`. With hazards on it builds the Sector Z ground (`grSectorMakeGround`) and a
process with a frame timer. Shot timers 0x10E, 0x97E, 0x987, ..., 0x9C6 (step 9), restart at 0x107D. Each shot plays FGM 0x109 and fires two
Arwing 2D lasers from (-3100, -230, 200) and (-3100, -230, -100) with `vel_air.x = -230` and the rotation
(-1.5708, -1.18396, 1.5708); the right laser only spawns if the left one did. The weapon descriptor is a copy of the Arwing laser descriptor
with the attributes offset moved from 0xBC to 0x130 in the stage header file (a `WPAttributes` with another model token and sound word).
With hazards off the stage builds nothing (the asm skips both the Sector Z ground and the Great Fox). The right shot's parent in the asm is the
Great Fox object; the left shot's parent register is undefined, so both pass the Great Fox object.

### 3.4 Jungle Japes 0x86 (134): water and KlapTrap

Asm `jungle_japes_setup`, `rushing_water`, `klaptrap_*`; port `grremixjapes.c`, `grremixhazards_japes.c` and `itremixklaptrap.c` (item 0x3A).
Setup (hazards on): water process, `itRemixKlapTrapSetStageFile(header)`, KlapTrap spawned at the origin.

- Rushing water: for each fighter below y = -1000: the first entry plays FGM 0x3D1 (not before the match starts); while the movement toggle is
  on and `status_id >= nFTCommonStatusWait` the fighter is pulled left, `x -= 90 - (x - base) * 0.0006`. The position is
  `coll_data.p_translate` (asm `player + 0x78`).
- KlapTrap: the item timer waits 484 frames, then each frame rolls 1 in 160 (hit value 80) and is forced at 600; a spawn restarts the timer, runs
  the joint track (file + 0x0908) and the material track (file + 0x0E20), and arms the hitbox (damage 20, angle 0x270, slash element 3, FGM
  0x105, bkb and kbs 0x100) the next frame. The hurtbox is off. At a blast zone the hitbox is disabled and x returns to the spawn point; the
  item is not destroyed, because the asm jumps past the `proc_dead` destroy.
- Deviation: `f20` (the base x of the pull) is a callee-saved FPU register that no routine of the asm sets. The port uses 0.0 (centre at x = 0),
  chosen from the routine it was copied from (Dream Land wind), not proven.

### 3.5 Game Boy Land 0x88 (136)

Asm `gbland_setup`, `gb_music_`; port `grremixgbland.c`. Skipped in training. A process counts frames: stop the music at frame 0, the Game Boy
beep (FGM 0x3D2) at 0xB4, then from 0x188 the stage songs: with the Medley as stage default (MIDI 0xA1) it plays once; with `Random Music` on it
plays the stage default once; otherwise the cue table (0x188 Muda 0x3C, beep at 0xCC8, Bubbly 0xA2 at 0xD40, beep 0x1880, Road to Cerulean City
0xA3 at 0x18F8, beep 0x2439, Level 1 Wario 0xA4 at 0x24B0, beep 0x2FF0, Mabe 0xA5 at 0x3068, beep 0x3BA8) and a restart at 0x3C20.
A new song only starts while the hammer (0x2D) or the star (0x2E) song is not playing. The MIDI ids come from the Remix MIDI bank order
(`docs/remix/audio.md`). No hazard toggle is read.

### 3.6 Pirate Land 0x9E (158)

Asm `pirate_land_setup`, `pirate_land_main_`, `cannon_main_`, `cannonball_*`; port `grremixpiratecannon.c` (setup and cannon),
`grremixpiratecannonball.c` (weapon kind 0x28). Hazards on: water through `grRemixWaterMakeGround(-600, -360)` and the cannon.

- Cannon: GObj with the joint descriptors at cannon file + 0x07A8, model at +0x0970, joint animation at +0x0BB4 and material animation at +0x0BF0
  (the cannon file is the stage header pointer at 0x80, the hitbox file the pointer at 0xAC). States Waiting, Turning and Firing; the barrel x is
  -128, 0 or +128; turn speed 4 (8 in a frenzy); waits 90 + rand(0..60) frames, 360 before the first shot, 30 between frenzy moves; a shot
  rolls a 5% chance of a frenzy of 10 + rand(0..10) moves; firing lasts 12 frames with the barrel scale 1.125, FGM 1012 (turn) and 1013 (shot),
  light screen shake.
- Cannonball: spawned at the base plus the barrel translate on the joint track of the position (0x0000, 0x0610, 0x0C50 in the hitbox file
  track table); `is_camera_follow` set, joint y rotation pi/2; it has no launch velocity (the properties struct with speed 100 is never
  referenced), the barrel animation moves it. Smoke puff (`efManagerDustExpandLargeMakeEffect`) every 9 frames. It explodes when its model
  animation ends (`anim_frame <= 0`; the 999-frame lifetime is a guard): sparkle effect, FGM 1, quake 1, hitbox size 360, damage 25, fire,
  angle 361, kbs 90, bkb 50, FGM 25, explosion lasts 15 frames. The explosion setup is the Samus bomb one (`wpSamusBombExplodeInitVars`) with the
  size line skipped, as the asm copies it.

### 3.7 Casino Night Zone 0x9F (159)

Asm `casino_night_setup`, `casino_night_side_bumper_bounce`. `grremixhazards_casino.c` spawns the stage bumper (`nITKindGBumper`, item 0x17)
at (0, 2600, 0) with hazards on and replaces its display list with the casino bumper list (stage ground data + 0x80 file, offset 0x2D0).
`grRemixCasinoWallBounceScale` (`grremixcasino.c`, hook in `ftCommonWallDamageSetStatus`): on 0x9F a wall hit on a surface above 0x16 (the side
bumpers) bounces with scale 1.1 and plays FGM 0x3D6, otherwise 0.8; the wall surface is the low byte of `lwall_flags` or `rwall_flags`
selected by `mask_curr` (asm 0x11F / 0x133 with the mask at 0xCE). The bumper hitbox is in section 2 (`peach_bumper_hitbox_`).

### 3.8 Metallic Madness 0xA2 (162): Robot Bee and laser

Asm `metallic_madness_setup`, `bee_factory_`, `shrink_timer_`, `robot_bee_*`, `robot_bee_laser_*`; port `grremixmetallic.c`,
`grremixmetallicbeelaser.c` (weapon kind 0x29), `itremixrobotbee.c` (item 0x3B). Hazards on only.

- Setup stores the stage header and the laser file (header + 0x130) and creates the two processes.
- Bee factory: counts frames once the match has started (always in Training); after 1500 frames it rolls 1 in 1500 (hit value 80) and forces a
  spawn at 3000. The bee spawns at the origin; the facing is drawn twice and the second value kept. Joint track file + 0x0908 (facing right) or
  + 0x3598 (left), material track + 0x09B4.
- Bee: lives 390 tics; inside the timer window 97 to 256 it fires the laser on a 13-tic repeat counter and plays the buzz FGM 0x3FB on a 48-tic
  counter; hitbox size 150, damage 5, angle 0x361, kbs 0x25, bkb 0x50, FGM 0x26; a hurtbox hit destroys it with FGM 0x3E8; it is destroyed at a
  blast zone.
- Laser: spawned 194 units to the side the bee faces and 336 below, speed (+-100, -100), Z rotation from `atan2f(vx, vy)`, transform
  `nGCMatrixKindTraRotRpyRSca` (the laser grows `scale.x` by 3 per frame towards the clamp); a map hit explodes it with the Fox blaster glow; a
  hit shrinks every fighter in the attack record that is not reflecting or absorbing (whole `FTStruct` flag word compared with the two asm
  patterns): `grRemixMetallicShrinkPlayer` sets the size multiplier to 0.5 for 900 tics and destroys an active Super Mushroom of that port; the
  shrink timer restores the size of the match state (`ftRemixSizeGetModeMultiplier`). The reflector flips the speed and re-aims; the absorb proc is
  `wpFoxBlasterProcHit`. Deviation: the maximum X scale of the laser is read by the asm from a vanilla-overlay float (RAM 0x8019CAA0) that the
  Remix RAM image does not contain; the port uses the Fox blaster clamp (`WPBLASTER_CLAMP_SCALE_X`, 160/3).

### 3.9 Rainbow Road 0xA3 (163): Chain Chomp

### 3.11 Onett 0x49 (73): Car

### 3.16 Time Twister 0xD2 (210), Scuttle Town 0xBF (191): background fades

Both create a ground GObj with an update process (priority 3) and a display callback (priority 6) that fills the rectangle (10, 10) to
(310, 230) with `G_RM_CLD_SURF` at the current RGB and alpha. The alpha steps by 4, the glass colour is written to ground data + 0x4C at alpha
0x80, the background bitmap is swapped at alpha 0xFC (`grRemixStageBgSetBitmap` writes the stage wallpaper `SObj`), and the transition ends at
alpha 0. Helpers in `grremixstagebg.c` (`grRemixStageBgBitmapToken`, `grRemixStageReadWarpBallBgIndex`, `grRemixStageReadFirstJointPaletteBits`).

- Time Twister (`grremixtimetwister.c`): the process waits for the warp ball (the joint four siblings after the first joint of the stage model, link 2) to be
  visible and reads its texture index as the next background (0 N. Sanity Beach 0x7AABD900, 1 Snow Go 0x4C82BD00, 2 Future Frenzy 0x5E729800,
  3 Time Twister 0x00005400; the four background headers are ground data +0xCC, +0xD0, +0xD4, +0x48). The fade alpha moves from frame 180: FGM
  0x94 and the song fade at 180, FGM 0x59E and the camera restore at 270, the new song at 300; FGM 0x59D and the camera zoom-out
  (`gmCameraSetStatusMapZoom` with the stage zoom bounds) when the transition starts. The song changes once per match and only when the stage
  plays the Time Twister track (`gmRemixBgmRulesTimeTwisterChanges`); the four tracks are in `gmremixbgmrules.c`. In Training the fade exists
  only with the Training stage-background option (`REMIX_TRAINING_STAGE_BG`). Time Twister SSS (0xD3) is a clone.
- Scuttle Town (`grremixscuttle.c`): the transition starts when the first joint palette index reads 1.0 (to night, background ground data
  + 0xC8, rgb 0x00105000) or 9.0 (to day, + 0x48, rgb 0x94CEFF00). Same Training rule. Deviation (mirrors the asm): `scuttle_town_setup_`
  has a `b _end` before the hazard block ("remove when minions stop crashing"), so the Remix build ships no Scuttle Minions;
  `GRREMIX_SCUTTLE_MINIONS_ENABLED` is FALSE. The minion port stays in `grremixscuttleminion.c` and `itremixscuttleminion.c` (item 0x3E:
  factory 1500-frame wait, 1 in 1500 roll, forced at 3000; hitbox size 150, damage 5; 390 tics of life) and is not reachable.

### 3.17 Peach's Castle II 0x44 (68): Banzai Bill
