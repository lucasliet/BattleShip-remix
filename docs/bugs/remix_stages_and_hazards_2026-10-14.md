# Remix stages: loader, stage select, music and hazards (2026-10-14)

Step 4 of the Remix roadmap. Status: IN PROGRESS. 173 of the 175 stage ids (0x29 to 0xD7) load and run in a VS match;
the stage select shows the Remix pages; the hazards of 15 stages are ported but only checked for load and crash.

## What exists

- **Loader.** `Stages.stage_file_table` is the vanilla `dMPCollisionGroundFileInfos` grown to the Remix ids: {map file id,
  header offset}. `grRemixGetGroundFileInfo` (`decomp/src/gr/grremix/grremixstage.c`) reads it from the Remix RAM image
  and `mpCollisionInitGroundData` uses it for gkind > 0x28. The port's gkind is the Remix stage id
  (`grremixids.h`, generated by `tools/gen_remix_stage_ids.py`). `tools/gen_remix_actions.py` exports the stage tables
  (`EXTRA_SYMBOLS`) and every `Hazards.*` symbol (`EXTRA_SYMBOL_PREFIXES`).
- **Ground maker.** `grMainSetupMakeGround` -> `grRemixMakeGround` dispatches on `Stages.function_table`: a vanilla maker
  address, `GRREMIX_FUNC_CLONE` (no hazards, 75 stages) or a Remix hazard routine, handed to `grRemixHazardsMakeGround`
  (`grremixhazards.c`, one `grremixhazards_<stage>.c` per stage).
- **Gating of the vanilla makers.** Remix keeps the vanilla maker for clones and patches the hazards inside it
  (`Hazards.asm` toggles, `mk_clone_hazard_removal_`, `congo_jungle_barrel_`). The port gates them under `REGION_REMIX`
  (`grremixgating.c`, `grjungle.c`, `grinishie.c`, `grzebes.c`, `grcastle.c`, ...). This fixed the crashes of the Mushroom
  Kingdom clones (65, 66, 155) and the Saffron clones (114, 118).
- **Wallpaper.** `Stages.bg_type`: every Remix stage uses the common wallpaper, Remix RTTF (0xBD) none
  (`grWallpaperMakeDecideKind`).
- **Music.** `grRemixGetStageBGM`: the `default_music_track_table` override, then the Occasional/Rare/Rare2 alternates by
  chance (65/16/10/9), applied in `mpCollisionInitGroundData` for VS.
- **Stage select.** `mnmaps.c` builds the pages at open from `Stages.stage_table_normal` (9 stages and Random per page, 10
  pages). Icons come from the Remix icon file (0xA04, `icon_offset_table`), names are typeset with the CSS font, preview
  zoom and translation come from `zoom_table`/`position_table`. Verified through the CSS flow: a custom stage (Mute City,
  page 3) starts a match.
- **Hazards ported (C, in `decomp/src/gr/grremix/`):** Great Bay and Twilight City water (`grremixwater.c`), Mad Monster
  Mansion, Big Boo's Haunt, Corneria (Great Fox), Game Boy Land, Pirate Land (cannon and cannonball), Jungle Japes with the
  KlapTrap item, Casino Night (stage bumper and side-bumper bounce), Onett with the Car item, Toad's Turnpike, Rainbow
  Road (Chain Chomp), Remix RTTF (bumper offset patch), Metallic Madness with the RobotBee item, Spawned Fear with the
  Cacodemon item and the Thunderball.

Fixes made while checking:

1. **Starmie crash** (`starmie_no_victim_2026-10-15.md`): found in the 5200-frame Onett run.
2. **KlapTrap** did not show: `itremixklaptrap.c` now hides the item at spawn (GObj camera tag 0), starts the joint track
   (file + 0x0908) and the material track (file + 0x0E20) at each respawn and shows it when the hitbox arms.
3. **Hazard intro check** shared as `grRemixHazardsMatchHasStarted()`; Great Bay, Twilight City and Pirate Land water now
   skip fighters before the match clock starts, as the asm does (Japes uses the same helper).

4. **Hazard item animations** (2026-10-16): the KlapTrap, Robot Bee and Cacodemon did not start their animation tracks.
   The base of the track offsets is not the stage header but the item pointer at header + 0xE0 minus 0x07E8 (the asm saves
   it at 0x801313F0): `grRemixHazardsItemFileTop`. KlapTrap swims across the water, the bee flies in from the upper left,
   the Cacodemon shoots its Thunderballs (its body stays at the screen edge in the camera framing used).
5. **Pirate Land cannonball** now explodes when its model animation ends (GObj `anim_frame <= 0`), not after 999 frames.

6. **T1.5 wave (2026-10-16).** Haiku workers ported Time Twister, Scuttle Town, ScuttleMinion, BulletBill, Peach Castle II,
   Peach/Casino bumper hitbox, Bowser's Stadium bombs (needs `BOWSER_BOMB_HEADER`, file 0x9B2, loaded in
   `grRemixBowserBombsStart` as the RTTF bomb item file), Yoshi's Island clouds and `platform_movement_`, Dream Land wind and
   downtime, Mushroom Kingdom hardcoding. Notes in `docs/remix/notes/f7_hazard_*.md`. Seen acting: Bowser's Stadium bombs.
   Scuttle Town minions are off in Remix 2.0.1 itself (`b _end` in `scuttle_town_setup_`); the port mirrors that after the
   minion item crashed in `gcSetupCustomDObjsWithMObj` (its file offset 0x40 does not match the standard footer layout).
